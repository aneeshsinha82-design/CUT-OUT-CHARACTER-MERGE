Cut Out Character Merge

Upload your black-background character videos. This page puts them into a hand-drawn sketch scene with a moving camera, shadows and an end card. Everything runs in your browser; your videos are never uploaded anywhere.

This browser cannot record video from a canvas. Please open this page in the latest Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1Add your character videos

Black background (pure black works best). They play in the order listed; files named 01, 02, 03… are sorted automatically.
Checking the black removal
Each clip shows a small preview on a grey checkerboard. Your person should look clean with no black patches inside the body. If edges look eaten, lower "Black-removal strength"; if you see dark specks around the person, raise it.

2Settings (optional, defaults are good)

Optional: your own landmark pictures and music

3Make the video

Making the video records it in real time, so a 30-second video takes about 30 seconds. Keep this tab open and in front while it runs.
